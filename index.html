FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SentinelIQ AI and what it means for your forecourt's safety compliance, staff retention, and operational efficiency.

What is SentinelIQ AI and why does my forecourt need it?

+
SentinelIQ AI is a predictive shift-safety governance platform designed specifically for UK petrol forecourts, convenience store franchises, and multi-site fuel retail businesses. Unlike legacy CCTV systems or passive panic buttons that only react after an incident, SentinelIQ AI screens every shift before it begins computing a Risk Score and Confidence Level based on staffing levels, historical site incidents, local crime indices, and regional police response times. This gives operators a proactive safety netting layer that protects lone workers, reduces escalation latency from 45 minutes to under 3 minutes, and builds legally auditable HSE compliance records that help negotiate lower insurance premiums.

How quickly can we deploy SentinelIQ AI at our forecourt?

+
Deployment is designed to be immediate. In Year 1, the platform intentionally bypasses complex POS or scheduling software API integrations instead using simple CSV or manual rota inputs. This means a pilot site can be fully operational within days, not weeks. Our baseline deployment measurement approach is specifically designed to gather clean, structured safety data from day one without waiting on corporate IT approvals. Full API integrations with dominant UK rota platforms (Deputy, Planday, Rotacloud) are introduced in Year 2 after pilot validation.

How does the Risk Score + Confidence Level system work?

+
Before every late-night lone-worker shift, the Shift-Risk Fingerprinting engine evaluates: (1) lone-worker status and staffing levels; (2) historical site-level incident patterns; (3) local crime indices (including the 2026 62% fuel theft surge data); and (4) regional emergency services response latencies. It then computes an objective Risk Score (Low / Medium / High) paired with a Confidence Level that indicates data completeness. If underlying data is sparse or offline, the Confidence Level drops explicitly alerting managers to data limitations and preventing a false sense of security. Every risk score is accompanied by a plain-language explanation (e.g., "High Risk: Solo evening shift during peak drive-off window with limited local visibility").

What happens when a lone worker misses a check-in?

+
The system automatically triggers a structured, tiered escalation protocol within seconds. This includes: (1) automated direct supervisor SMS/push notification; (2) launch of emergency backup workflows; and (3) preparation of a structured incident dossier for compliance records. The entire process shrinks escalation latency from an average of 45 minutes of manual supervisor calling to under 2.2 minutes. Participating forecourts in our pilot programme demonstrated a 92% average reduction in missed check-in response times.

Does the Manager Override feature compromise safety?

+
No the Manager Override Feature is specifically designed to preserve managerial agency while building a legally auditable compliance trail. If a manager must override a high-risk shift warning (e.g., due to unavoidable staffing constraints), the system enforces a mandatory, structured reason-capture workflow. The manager's qualitative reasoning is permanently logged alongside the shift data creating an unalterable audit record that can be presented to HSE inspectors and used to demonstrate that all "reasonably practicable" steps were taken to ensure worker safety.

How does SentinelIQ AI help reduce insurance premiums?

+
Commercial insurers increasingly require demonstrable evidence of active safety governance to justify premium reductions. SentinelIQ AI generates a continuous, structured audit trail of: (1) pre-shift risk assessments with objective scores; (2) 100% logged manager override reasons; (3) verified check-in compliance records; and (4) documented escalation response times. By presenting this data to insurers as a legally defensible risk mitigation history, operators can directly negotiate lower liability and business interruption insurance premiums delivering a measurable financial return within the 8-to-12-week pilot window.

Is SentinelIQ AI compliant with UK GDPR and HSE regulations?

+
Yes. SentinelIQ AI is built on a privacy-by-design framework fully compliant with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) standards and UK GDPR. The platform does not engage in continuous location tracking or audio surveillance it operates on discrete scheduling metadata and active check-in confirmations, ensuring strict data minimisation. For HSE compliance, the platform is specifically designed to support operators in demonstrating active adherence to lone-working guidelines INDG73 and INDG424, with every shift screening and override event creating an unalterable, legally defensible audit trail.
